# Experimental driver assets

The driver is a derivative assembled and posed for OpenGrid XR. These assets
retain their own licenses; the application's license does not replace them.

- **Gloves and finger poses — Valve Corporation.** SteamVR Unity Plugin,
  revision `056c82369d78f253af8cefcae9b289efd69bd960`, slim right glove,
  original red diffuse/normal maps and authored StickGrab/Rest finger poses.
  BSD three-clause license in `VALVE-LICENSE.txt`.
  https://github.com/ValveSoftware/steamvr_unity_plugin/tree/056c82369d78f253af8cefcae9b289efd69bd960/Assets/SteamVR/Models
  Changes: mirrored left hand, wheel-specific placement, pose blending,
  palm-anchored wrist relief (authored finger shapes retained), subdivided rest
  surface, offline skinning conversion and optional runtime color themes.
- **Racing suit — Elvaerwyn, “Elvs Racing Fire suit Male1”.** Official MakeHuman
  suits03 asset pack, Creative Commons Attribution. The pack's licensing FAQ
  links CC BY 2.5 Sweden: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.5/se/.
  Original pack: https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/suits03.html
  License FAQ: https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/faq.html
  Changes: seated fitting, separate arms/torso/legs, continuous sleeve
  retopology with sampled garment variation and gentle authored folds,
  arm-only weighting, tapered wrist-aligned cuffs, closed shoulder ends and
  OpenGrid color themes. Original suit logos/textures are not included.
- **Body rig foundation — MakeHuman community.** Graphical assets at revision

  Only graphical rig data is used; no MakeHuman program code is incorporated.

`driver-source.blend` contains the editable rig/meshes and packed glove maps.
`grasp-poses.json` retains the authored Valve finger-pose data. Regenerate each
`.agd` with Blender 4.5:

```
blender --background --python scripts/cook-driver.py -- --model ascher-artura
blender --background --python scripts/cook-driver.py -- --model fpe2
```

Runtime files are immutable geometry, weights and precomputed steering palettes;
the five built-in GSI wheels share `fpe2.agd`, while Artura uses its own fit.
Blender is an authoring dependency only. Handover starts at 70 degrees, the
other hand takes over at 90 degrees. This is a pose sampler, not physical hand
tracking. Torso/legs are optional seated meshes, not tracked body parts.

The version 3 cook preserves garment UVs and includes the packed Valve normal
map after the diffuse atlas. Subdivision is baked into glove and sleeve rest
surfaces; optional torso/legs retain their original mesh density. Sleeve and
hand wrist axes are blended, with a bounded root adjustment about a fixed palm
anchor when needed to avoid extreme wrist flexion. Minor finger/wheel overlap
can remain during handover; these are authored poses, not collision simulation.

Version 4 adds original OpenGrid racing-fabric artwork generated by
`scripts/driver_cloth.py`: neutral woven panels, stitching and compression
wrinkles, plus small sleeve-shape adjustments during the cook. The shared
2048 x 1024 diffuse and normal atlases retain a 1024-square glove region and a
separate cloth region. Cloth diffuse alpha is a theme-independent stitch mask,
not transparency. No original suit logos or third-party fabric images are used.
At load time the regions become separate texture-array slices with independent
mip levels, preventing cross-material color or stitching bleed at distance.
